Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

A few things you might want to know before getting in touch.

How do I request a commission?

Send me a message on Instagram or an email with your idea, references and the type of artwork you have in mind. We’ll discuss the details before you decide.

How much does a commission cost?

Pricing depends on the type of artwork, complexity and details. I’ll share a quote after understanding your request, before any work begins.

Can I request anime, chibi or cartoon styles?

Yes! You can request character artwork in anime, chibi or cartoon-inspired styles. Share a reference or describe the look you want.

How long will my artwork take?

It depends on the artwork and my current workload. I’ll discuss an estimated timeline with you before confirming the commission.

Do you offer revisions?

We’ll discuss the brief and expectations before I start. Any revision arrangements will be explained when we agree on the commission.

How will I receive the finished artwork?

We’ll agree on the delivery format before the commission begins. Digital artwork can be delivered as a digital file.
