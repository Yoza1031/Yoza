01 / ABOUT ME
A little about who I am.

I’m Dilshan, an artist and student from India. I love exploring different ways to create, learning new things and turning little ideas into drawings.

02 / HOW I FELL IN LOVE WITH ART
How I fell in love with art.

I drew occasionally as I grew up, and started again during the 2021 lockdown, but that wasn’t when I fell in love with art. That happened in 2024. I became so obsessed with drawing that it turned into a part of my everyday life. And here I am today. I hope this feeling stays with me for the rest of my life.
