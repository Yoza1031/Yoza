01 / ABOUT ME
A little about who I am.

I’m Dilshan Singh, an 18-year-old student from India. I’m an artist who loves exploring different ways to create, learning new things and turning little ideas into drawings.

02 / HOW IT STARTED
It began with a pencil.

I first picked up a pencil when I was around six or seven, following step-by-step drawing guides without knowing where it would lead. I kept drawing occasionally over the years, then returned to it during the 2021 lockdown. In 2024, I truly fell in love with it. Drawing became part of my everyday life, and I started experimenting with different mediums, from watercolours to alcohol markers. There’s still so much I want to learn, and I hope I’ll keep drawing for the rest of my life.
